Generated by `python -m fraud.threshold` and `python -m fraud.final_eval`. Costs are in the currency units of TransactionAmt and rest on illustrative assumptions: a missed fraud costs its amount, a false alarm costs a fixed review fee, and a caught fraud costs nothing. Thresholds are chosen on validation only.
